How the model works & limitations

Independent Poisson goal model. For each team, the full-match scoring rate is 60% recent attack/opponent defence average, 25% venue attack/opponent venue defence average, and 15% head-to-head scoring average. Missing venue or head-to-head samples fall back to recent scoring. Remaining rates scale by (90 − entered minute) / 90. Current goals are added to future score probabilities.

Provider analysis uses up to 10 most recent completed matches per team and 5 head-to-head games from the loaded current and previous seasons, strictly before the selected kickoff. Manual mode uses your supplied averages. Red cards, injuries, lineups, shots, xG, stoppage time and tactical changes are not modelled. This is an uncalibrated baseline; no accuracy claim or betting guarantee. Displayed sample counts describe coverage, not confidence.
