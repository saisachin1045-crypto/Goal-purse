Methodology

We weight recent games more heavily (0.90 decay per game) and average each side’s goals scored with the opponent’s goals conceded over up to ten previous games. Venue-specific results and up to five direct meetings contribute when available. We blend that estimate with opponent-adjusted attack and defence ratings fitted together from earlier results, with a 120-day half-life and sparse-team ratings pulled toward average. A Poisson model scales those rates to the regulation minutes remaining. The minute is estimated from kickoff assuming a 15-minute break and constrained by the reported half. You can adjust it. Forecasts are conditional on that minute and the displayed score. Probability estimates have not been calibrated against held-out results. A five-feature vector compares team scoring rates, minute, score difference and total goals against recent settled forecasts. With at least ten similar records, the remaining home and away rates adjust by up to 20%. The model excludes stoppage time, cards and substitutions.

Source: Flashscore · Parimatch listings unverified.
